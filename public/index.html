I’m a Physics PhD student at University of California, Riverside. My research focuses on using Computer Simulations, Data Analysis techniques, and Machine Learning to study ground-based Gravitational-wave detectors. Our group (Richardson Lab @ UCR) is developing new technologies to address fundamental challenges in gravitational-wave detection, both for the next upgrades of Advanced LIGO, and the next generation of gravitational-wave detectors.
